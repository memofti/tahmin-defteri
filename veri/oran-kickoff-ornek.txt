Our Betting Markets
We’ve made sure to cover all of the biggest markets, to give you the best chance of placing a successful sports bet.
Draw No Bet
Draw no bet
works by removing the option of the game ending in a draw entirely. This means that you’ll get your stake returned if it does.
With the right strategy you can expect to make a tasty profit. The added level of protection is also extremely attractive to some bettors.
Correct Score
Football matches can be highly unpredictable, therefore you can find some highly lucrative odds in this market.
Our
correct score tips
are updated regularly, pulling information and statistics from a variety of sources to give you the best chance of success. We take into account everything from current form to match history in order to bring you all you need to make an informed bet.
First Goalscorer
Depending on the match you’re betting on, this market can be home to some attractive odds.
Our
first goalscorer tips
are based on the most likely player, taking into account a range of data and figures. The winning bet must accurately predict the first player to find the back of the net.
Anytime Goalscorer
Anytime goalscorer odds
aren’t as high as the first goal scorer market due to greater predictability, but there are still big profits to be found. This is particularly true if you’re placing an acca bet.
Win to Nil
Win to nil
bets require your selected team to claim victory while also keeping a clean sheet. If they win but concede in the process, unfortunately it’s a losing bet.
So, in order to win, both parts of your prediction must be correct.
Double Chance
Place a
double chance bet
to cover two out of three possible outcomes within a football match. This gives you a level of security. To claim a big win in this market, it’s recommended to place an acca bet, maximising the odds and giving yourself the best shot at a tasty profit.

Both teams to score
is a very straightforward market, as you’re simply betting on both sides to score a goal over the full 90 minutes.
Both teams to score and win
differs slightly – as you must also bet on which team you think will be victorious.
