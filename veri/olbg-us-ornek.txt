Football Tips FAQs
Who is the best football tipster?
The best football tipster is all about preference. If you are talking about the best English Premier League tipster, then for the 2025-26 season, that was
Isibiki
, who returned a profit of +516 (to 10pt stakes) in the 1X2 market. Perhaps you want a Correct Scores tipster? Ahead of the new season (2026-27), for the 12 months prior,

best football tipsters page

Should you pay for football tips?
No, you should never pay for football tips, because you can access so many excellent tips here at OLBG for free.
Can you make money from betting on football?
You can make money betting on football, but professional football bettors are less common than professional horse racing bettors. To win consistently, you need to find value bets regularly, which means finding an edge over the market. This is far harder for football, as much of the form and the reasons for finding a good bet are exposed, and the betting margins are tighter.
How do you pick a winning football bet?
You pick a winning football bet after skilful analysis of the form. There are many angles to study, such as team form and head-to-head records, but also team formations, player fitness and how different selections will impact the game. Visit our
Bookie Guides: Learning
section to understand some of the angles that OLBG members use to find a winning football bet.
What is the best football prediction site?
We'd argue OLBG is the best football prediction site, because here you can benefit from the knowledge of hundreds of expert football tipsters who specialise in different leagues and markets and have different strategies for finding winners. You can find great tipsters to follow but, more importantly, you can learn by understanding their betting tactics. The proof is in the records — every tipster's profit, strike rate and consistency is there for you to analyse, so you can judge the quality for yourself rather than taking our word for it.
How do I win a football bet every time?
You will not win a bet every time, and there are no sure-fire, guaranteed-win bets. The way to profit from betting is to bet at value odds as consistently as possible — betting at odds greater than a selection's true chance of winning — and by doing that, you will turn the odds in your favour.
